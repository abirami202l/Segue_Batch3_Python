# Set 3 – Hospital Appointment Booking Application
## Task 1: Product Vision and Stakeholders

### Product vision
For patients who need to consult doctors at a hospital in the group, the Hospital Appointment Booking Application is a mobile-friendly service that allows them to find available doctors, book or change appointments and receive reminders. Unlike relying only on telephone calls or visiting the hospital to enquire, the application provides a convenient view of appointment availability and booking details, helping patients plan visits and helping hospital staff manage schedules.

### Product goal
Provide a first release that lets patients find a doctor, book an available appointment, reschedule or cancel within the hospital's rules, and receive appointment reminders.

### Stakeholders and Scrum involvement

| Stakeholder | Main interest | Scrum involvement |
|---|---|---|
| Patients | Easy booking, accurate slot information, clear reminders and simple cancellation. | **Customers/users:** Provide feedback, validate usability and participate in reviews or user testing. |
| Hospital management | Better appointment utilisation and service quality. | Stakeholder who communicates business goals and reviews progress; may nominate or support the Product Owner. |
| Doctors | Accurate schedules and manageable appointment loads. | Domain stakeholders who confirm schedule rules and review whether proposed functionality fits clinical operations. |
| Reception and appointment staff | Avoid duplicate bookings and reduce manual coordination. | Operational stakeholders who explain current processes and validate workflows. |
| Product Owner | Maximise product value and order requirements. | **Product Owner:** Accountable for the Product Goal and Product Backlog. A hospital representative with authority to make product trade-offs may fill this role. |
| Scrum Master | Help the team use Scrum effectively and resolve impediments. | **Scrum Master:** Facilitates Scrum and supports team effectiveness; should not be treated as the project secretary. |
| Cross-functional delivery team | Design, build, test and integrate the application. | **Developers:** Accountable for creating a usable Increment. Skills may include UI, application development, testing and integration. |

### Important distinction
Stakeholders are not automatically Scrum accountabilities. Patients, doctors, hospital managers and reception staff provide needs, constraints and feedback. The Product Owner, Scrum Master and Developers hold the three Scrum accountabilities. The Developers may consult stakeholders directly, while the Product Owner remains accountable for Product Backlog ordering and value decisions.
