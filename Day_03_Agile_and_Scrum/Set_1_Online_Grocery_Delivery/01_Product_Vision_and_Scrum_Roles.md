# Set 1 – Online Grocery Delivery Application
## Task 1: Product Vision and Scrum Roles

### Product vision
For households in the supermarket chain's regional service area who need a convenient way to purchase everyday groceries, the Online Grocery Delivery Application is a mobile-friendly ordering service that lets customers discover products, place orders and follow home deliveries. Unlike phone-based ordering or travelling to a store, it provides a clear catalogue, a simple checkout process and delivery updates, helping customers save time while allowing the supermarket to expand its digital sales.

### Product goal
Launch a reliable minimum viable product (MVP) that allows a registered customer to browse available groceries, add items to a cart, place an order for an available delivery slot and view the order's delivery status.

### Scrum accountabilities and anti-patterns

| Scrum role | Main accountability | Example anti-pattern |
|---|---|---|
| Product Owner | Maximises product value and manages the Product Backlog, including ordering and clarity of items. | Acts as a requirements secretary who accepts every stakeholder request without making value-based decisions. |
| Scrum Master | Establishes Scrum, helps the team and organisation understand it, and removes or helps resolve impediments. | Acts as a traditional project manager who assigns tasks and controls the Developers' daily work. |
| Developers | Create a usable Increment each Sprint and manage the work needed to meet the Sprint Goal. | Work in isolated silos, hand work over between specialists and avoid shared responsibility for the Increment. |

### Scope assumptions
- The first release serves one regional supermarket chain and its defined delivery area.
- Customers use a supported mobile or web interface and have an account.
- Product prices and availability are maintained by the supermarket.
- Online payment is included in the initial backlog; payment processing is assumed to use an approved payment provider.
- The MVP supports one delivery address per order. Advanced loyalty schemes and multiple-store fulfilment are outside the initial release.
