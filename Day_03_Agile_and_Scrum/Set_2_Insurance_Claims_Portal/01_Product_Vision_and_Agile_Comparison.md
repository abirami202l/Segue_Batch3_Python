# Set 2 – Insurance Claims Portal
## Task 1: Product Vision and Waterfall-versus-Agile Comparison

### Product vision
For policyholders who need to report and follow an insurance claim, the Insurance Claims Portal is a secure self-service service that guides customers through claim registration, document submission and settlement updates. Unlike paper forms, email exchanges and repeated phone enquiries, the portal gives customers a consistent way to submit information and see the current claim status, while helping the insurer reduce avoidable manual follow-up.

### Product goal
Deliver an initial portal through which a customer can register a claim, upload required supporting documents, view claim progress and receive settlement notifications.

### Waterfall versus Agile

| Aspect | Waterfall | Agile |
|---|---|---|
| Requirements | Defined in detail near the beginning. | Refined continuously as the team learns. |
| Delivery | Usually delivered in larger phases or near the end. | Delivered in small, usable increments. |
| Feedback | Often received at formal milestone reviews. | Collected regularly during Sprint Reviews and refinement. |
| Change | Changes can be costly after a phase is approved. | Change is expected and managed through backlog ordering. |
| Testing | Often concentrated after development phases. | Testing and quality checks occur throughout each Sprint. |
| Risk | Important usability or process issues may be discovered late. | Early increments expose risks and assumptions sooner. |

### Why Agile suits this programme
Insurance claim processes include different claim types, document requirements and customer expectations. Agile allows the insurer to validate a basic registration journey early, obtain feedback from claims staff and customers, and adjust the backlog as regulatory or operational needs become clearer. Incremental delivery also makes it possible to test document upload and status visibility before the complete portal is finished. Security, privacy and compliance remain mandatory quality constraints in every Sprint; Agile does not remove the need for governance.
